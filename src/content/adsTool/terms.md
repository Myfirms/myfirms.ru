## 1. About the Tool

MyFirms Ads API Tool (the "Tool") is an internal tool operated by MyFirms at [myfirms.ru](https://myfirms.ru). It uses the Google Ads API to report on and manage Google Ads campaigns for accounts that we manage ourselves or for our clients. It is available only to our team and to clients who have authorized us to access their Google Ads accounts.

## 2. Authorization

The Tool accesses a Google Ads account only after an authorized user of that account signs in with Google and grants access. You may revoke access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 3. Acceptable use

The Tool may be used only to manage Google Ads accounts the user is authorized to manage, in compliance with the [Google Ads API Terms and Conditions](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/api-policy/terms) and Google Ads policies. Campaign changes that spend budget are made only on explicit instruction of the account owner.
